Canvas

An SVG canvas of HTML components, each in a foreignObject, and shapes drawn between them. The bundle picks which components: the notebook's, or with ?bundle=llm the parts of an LLM request and response (messages, tool definitions, calls and results, structured output, JSON and JSON Schema). With ▢, click anywhere to put a hole there and press its + to pick what it becomes, maybe a vertical box, horizontal box, grid or tabs holding holes of its own. An item's chrome shows while the pointer is over it or something in it has the focus (a collapsed one keeps its header): give it a title in its header, drag it by its header, resize it by its corner, collapse it with ▾, remove it with ✕, and toggle editing a layout with ✎ (adding, removing and resizing its slots) and back to using it with 👁. The other tools draw lines, arrows, rectangles (Shift: squares), ellipses (Shift: circles) and text; ⬉ selects and moves shapes, Delete removes the selected one.
