Canvas

An SVG canvas of HTML components, each in a foreignObject, and shapes drawn between them. With ▢, click anywhere to put a hole there and press its + to pick what it becomes; drag an item by its header, resize it by its corner, collapse it with ▾, remove it with ✕. The other tools draw lines, arrows, rectangles (Shift: squares), ellipses (Shift: circles) and text; ⬉ selects and moves shapes, Delete removes the selected one.
